THE CLINICAL BREAKTHROUGH

Redefining Margin Assessment in Breast-Conserving Surgery

When pathology results arrive days after surgery, patients can face the emotional and clinical trauma of a second operation. Coosa’s Breast Cancer Detector (BCD) brings the decision into the operating room by analyzing catalytic Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) and cellular metabolic markers with multi-wall carbon nanotube micro-sensor arrays.

The result is actionable intraoperative margin verification in seconds—before the wound is closed, and without compromising the specimen for definitive pathology.

ULTRASOUND BIOPSY GUIDANCE
Pre-Operative Stratification

BIRADx System (BDS)

Real-Time Probe-Guided Biopsy Stratification Platform[cite: 2]

Designed for real-time tissue characterization under direct ultrasound guidance[cite: 2]. The BIRADx probe differentiates ambiguous BI-RADS 3 breast lesions from high-risk BI-RADS 4+ pathologies before incision, resolving screening challenges in dense fibroglandular tissue[cite: 2].

  • Sensitivity: 95.0% verified clinical sensitivity[cite: 2]
  • Specificity: 89.0% diagnostic specificity[cite: 2]
  • Overall Accuracy: 90.0% across clinical validation trials (n=138 masses)[cite: 2]
  • Guidance Modality: High-resolution ultrasound biopsy tracking[cite: 2]
INTRAOPERATIVE SURGICAL SUITE
Intraoperative Surgical Probe

Breast Cancer Detector (BCD)

Real-Time Margin & Lymph Node Assessment System[cite: 2, 3]

An intraoperative surgical probe used directly within lumpectomy cavities and axillary lymph nodes during breast-conserving surgery[cite: 2, 3]. Detects microscopic positive margins by measuring metabolic ROS excretion in real-time, preserving specimen architecture for later pathology[cite: 3, 6].

  • Margin Sensitivity: 97.0% intraoperative margin sensitivity[cite: 3]
  • Margin Selectivity: 94.0% selectivity along surgical cavity borders[cite: 3]
  • Axillary Nodes: 91.0% sensitivity in nodal assessment[cite: 3]
  • Clinical Impact: Reduces positive post-surgical margins by ~30%[cite: 3]
